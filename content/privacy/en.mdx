# Privacy policy

The website [nopasaran.ch](https://nopasaran.ch) is published by Rebel Suisse. This page describes, in general terms, the data that may be processed when you use the site.

## Visiting the site

The site can be visited freely, with no account or registration. It is hosted by technical providers that may record some connection data, such as your IP address or browser type, to keep the site running and secure.

## Statistics

We use an audience measurement tool that gives us general visit statistics. It does not set cookies.

## Cookies

The site does not use advertising cookies. A technical cookie may be used to remember the language you chose.

## Contact form

If you write to us through the contact form, the information you send (for example your email address, your message and any attachments) goes through a form delivery provider. We use it only to handle your message.

## Social networks

We publish the site's content on our social media accounts, sometimes automatically. These posts do not involve collecting any data about the site's visitors. The platforms concerned apply their own privacy rules.

For any question: contact@nopasaran.ch
